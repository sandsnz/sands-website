Support resources

These resources from Sands New Zealand include free downloadable pamphlets, information about support packs and the Certificate of Life.

What's in a support pack

A support pack contains information pamphlets, inkless kits and special memory booklets. These items can be an important part of the memory-building process, as there is often so little time available to spend with our babies.

Packs are provided through hospitals and midwives in many regions (Ministry of Health–funded). Local groups and the national network can also help families and LMCs obtain packs. Contact us or your local group if you would like a pack or more information.

Certificate of Life

Every pēpi | baby, no matter how briefly they were with us, deserves to be recognised. The Certificate of Life is available to all whānau who have experienced any form of pregnancy or baby loss. It can be especially meaningful for whānau whose baby died before 20 weeks, when their baby’s birth cannot be formally registered and no birth certificate is issued.

What it is

The Certificate of Life is a personal keepsake that honours your baby and the place they hold in your whānau. It is not a legal document, but a tangible acknowledgement of your baby’s life. Many whānau find comfort in seeing their baby’s name recorded and having something they can keep and treasure.

Certificates are available through hospitals and Sands groups, and can also be ordered from Sands New Zealand.

How to get one

  • Ask your midwife, hospital or local Sands group
  • Or contact Sands NZ — resources@sands.org.nz
  • Or download one here
